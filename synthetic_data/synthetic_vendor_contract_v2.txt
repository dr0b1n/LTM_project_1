1. Parties and Services
1.1 Parties
This Vendor Services Agreement is entered into between Northstar Demo Services, the Vendor, and Example Customer Organization, the Customer, solely as synthetic material for a software demonstration. The Vendor will provide hosted document collaboration, managed storage, customer support, and related implementation services.

1.2 Service Contact
The Vendor service contact is Alex Morgan. Correspondence may be sent to alex.morgan@example.test or +1 202-555-0147. The designated demonstration server address is 192.0.2.25.

1.3 Customer Data
Customer Data means information submitted to the hosted service by or on behalf of the Customer. The Vendor will process Customer Data only to provide, secure, maintain, and support the contracted services, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

1.4 Confidentiality
Each party will use reasonable safeguards to protect the other party's confidential information and will disclose such information only to personnel and service providers who require access for the purposes of this Agreement.

2. Information Security
2.1 Security Program
The Vendor will maintain a written information security program containing administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of Customer Data and the hosted services.

2.2 Encryption at Rest
The Vendor shall encrypt all Customer Data stored within Vendor-managed systems using AES-256 encryption or an equivalent industry-standard encryption method.

2.3 Encryption in Transit
The Vendor shall protect Customer Data transmitted over public networks using TLS 1.2 or a later supported version, or an equivalent secure transport protocol.

2.4 Access Control
Access to production systems will be limited according to job responsibilities. Privileged access will be reviewed periodically and removed when no longer required.

2.5 Vulnerability Management
The Vendor will operate a vulnerability management process that includes regular scanning, risk-based remediation, and periodic review of identified security weaknesses.

3. Security Incidents
3.1 Incident Handling
The Vendor will maintain procedures to identify, investigate, contain, and remediate confirmed security incidents affecting Customer Data.

3.2 Notification Commitment
The Vendor shall notify the Customer of a confirmed security incident within thirty calendar days after completing its internal investigation.

3.3 Notification Contents
When available, a notification will describe the known nature of the incident, the categories of affected Customer Data, containment measures taken, and reasonable actions recommended to the Customer.

3.4 Cooperation
The Vendor will provide reasonable cooperation and periodic updates while material remediation activities remain in progress.

4. Data Management
4.1 Data Retention
The Vendor may retain Customer Data for a reasonable period following termination where necessary for operational, legal, backup, or dispute-resolution purposes.

4.2 Return of Data
Upon written request made before termination, the Vendor will make a standard export of Customer Data available to the Customer in a commonly used electronic format.

4.3 Deletion
After the applicable retention period expires, the Vendor will delete or render inaccessible Customer Data from active systems. Residual backup copies will be removed through the Vendor's ordinary backup rotation process.

4.4 Data Accuracy
The Customer remains responsible for the accuracy, quality, and legality of Customer Data submitted to the service.

5. Subprocessors
5.1 Authorized Use
The Customer authorizes the Vendor to engage subprocessors that are necessary to deliver hosting, support, monitoring, and communications services.

5.2 General Notice
The Vendor will publish an updated list of material subprocessors through its customer portal and will provide at least thirty days prior notice before appointing a new material subprocessor.

5.3 Urgent Appointment
Notwithstanding Section 5.2, the Vendor may appoint a replacement subprocessor without prior notice when the Vendor determines that an urgent operational or security need exists. Notice will be provided when reasonably practicable after the appointment.

5.4 Subprocessor Obligations
The Vendor will require each material subprocessor to protect Customer Data under written obligations appropriate to the services performed.

6. Service Continuity and General Terms
6.1 Availability
The Vendor will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the hosted service available each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance, emergency maintenance, and events beyond the Vendor's reasonable control.

6.2 Business Continuity
The Vendor will maintain documented business continuity and disaster recovery procedures and will exercise those procedures periodically.

6.3 Recovery Objectives
Recovery priorities and restoration sequencing will be determined according to the affected service and incident conditions. Specific recovery time and recovery point commitments are not stated in this Agreement.

6.4 Term and Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for a material breach that remains uncured thirty days after written notice, unless a shorter period is required to prevent ongoing harm.

6.5 Entire Agreement
This Agreement and its referenced schedules constitute the entire agreement concerning the services and supersede prior discussions on the same subject.
