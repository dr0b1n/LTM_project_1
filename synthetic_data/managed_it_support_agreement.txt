=== PAGE 2 ===
2. Customer Responsibilities

The Customer will maintain software licenses, identify authorized contacts and provide timely access to supported systems. Harborline may rely on instructions from listed contacts.
The Customer remains responsible for approving material production changes and maintaining business-owned backups unless a work order says otherwise.
- Provide accurate asset inventory.
- Report suspected incidents promptly.
- Review monthly service reports.

=== PAGE 3 ===
3. Personnel and Confidentiality

Harborline personnel will keep Customer confidential information confidential and use it only to provide services. Personnel receive general security awareness training.
Harborline performs screening where permitted by law and appropriate to the assigned role, but this Agreement does not define minimum screening checks.
NOTE: Personnel screening is described without a measurable minimum.

=== PAGE 4 ===
4. Accounts and Remote Administration

Technicians should use named support identities where technically practical. Legacy systems may use a shared support credential held in the team's approved password vault.
Remote administration uses encrypted support tooling. Multi-factor authentication is enabled for Harborline's central support portal, but the Agreement does not expressly require MFA for every privileged connection to Customer systems.
- Access is removed when personnel leave the support team.
- The support manager reviews group membership from time to time.

=== PAGE 5 ===
5. Security Controls

Harborline will use commercially reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect Customer information during service delivery.
The Agreement does not state an encryption algorithm for stored support records or a minimum network protocol for all data transfers.
NOTE: This section intentionally lacks measurable encryption commitments.

=== PAGE 6 ===
6. Logs and Vulnerability Handling

The support platform records ticket changes and technician sign-in activity. Logs are retained according to Harborline's standard operational schedule.
Harborline will address security weaknesses in tools it manages in a commercially reasonable period, taking severity and operational impact into account.
NOTE: Logging and remediation language is intentionally non-measurable.

=== PAGE 7 ===
7. Incident Handling

Harborline will inform the Customer promptly after Harborline determines that a security event materially affected Customer information.
The parties will cooperate on containment and recovery. No fixed notification period is stated in this Agreement.
- Initial facts may be incomplete.
- The Customer remains responsible for regulatory notifications unless otherwise agreed.

=== PAGE 8 ===
8. Service Levels and Continuity

Priority-one tickets receive a response target of one hour. This response target does not guarantee service restoration.
Harborline maintains general continuity procedures for its service desk, but the Agreement does not state an exercise frequency, recovery time objective or recovery point objective.
NOTE: Ticket response is not the same as a disaster-recovery commitment.

=== PAGE 9 ===
9. Subcontractors and Locations

Harborline may use subcontractors to provide after-hours support. Harborline remains responsible for their performance and requires confidentiality obligations.
Harborline may change subcontractors without advance notice. The Agreement does not expressly require equivalent security obligations or specify permitted data-processing countries.
- Current subcontractor information is available on request.

=== PAGE 10 ===
10. Termination and Data Handling

Following termination, Harborline will provide available open-ticket records to the Customer on request and remove Customer access from the support portal.
Harborline will delete Customer information when no longer reasonably needed for business, legal or operational purposes. The Agreement does not define a deletion deadline or a backup-removal period.
This synthetic agreement is intended only for demonstration and contains no real business information.
NOTE: Return and deletion commitments are intentionally incomplete.
