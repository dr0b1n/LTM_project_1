# Synthetic Vendor Compliance Dataset

This package contains three fictional, text-based vendor agreements, matching plain-text sources, one 12-policy baseline, and expected findings for demonstration evaluation.

Contracts:
- cloud_analytics_agreement: 12 pages, balanced mix of compliant, non-compliant, ambiguous and conflicting terms.
- managed_it_support_agreement: 10 pages, deliberately incomplete and vague in several control areas.
- customer_support_saas_agreement: 14 pages, stronger controls with selected exceptions and one prompt-injection-style test sentence.

All names, organizations, contact details and contract terms are fictional. The files are not legal advice and must not be used as real agreements.

Expected findings are evaluation data. Do not include them in embedding or local-model prompts.
