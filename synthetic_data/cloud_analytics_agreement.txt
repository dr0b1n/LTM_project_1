=== PAGE 1 ===
1. Parties, Purpose and Definitions

This Cloud Analytics Services Agreement governs Northstar Metrics Ltd.'s hosted analytics service for Ellison Retail Group. The service receives sales, inventory, support and workforce-planning data supplied by the Customer.
Customer Data means information uploaded to or generated through the service. Security Incident means a confirmed compromise of Customer Data confidentiality, integrity or availability. Material Subprocessor means a third party that stores Customer Data or performs a security-sensitive processing activity.

=== PAGE 2 ===
2. Service Scope and Processing Instructions

Northstar will process Customer Data only to provide analytics, dashboards, forecasting and contracted support, and only on documented Customer instructions unless law requires otherwise.
Customer controls source-system configuration and the accuracy of uploaded data. Northstar may use aggregated statistics that cannot reasonably identify the Customer or a person to improve service performance.
- Processing locations are listed in Schedule 2.
- Service changes may not materially reduce the contracted security controls.
- Personnel with Customer Data access are bound by confidentiality obligations.

=== PAGE 3 ===
3. Security Program and Encryption

Northstar maintains a documented security program appropriate to the service risk. Customer Data is encrypted at rest using AES-256. Encryption keys are managed through a centralized key-management service and production key access is restricted.
Customer Data transmitted over public or untrusted networks is protected using TLS 1.2 or later. Deprecated protocols are disabled for production service endpoints.
- Annual security-risk assessment
- Secure configuration standards
- Separation of production and test environments

=== PAGE 4 ===
4. Identity and Privileged Access

Each workforce user receives a unique identity. Access is granted according to job responsibilities and least privilege. Application and infrastructure access is reviewed quarterly, with unnecessary access removed.
Privileged and remote administrative access requires multi-factor authentication and approval by an authorized service owner. Emergency privileged access is time-limited and reviewed after use.
- Shared administrator accounts are prohibited except for documented technical service accounts.
- Terminated-user access is disabled by the end of the termination day.

=== PAGE 5 ===
5. Logging, Monitoring and Vulnerability Management

Northstar logs authentication, administrative activity, security alerts and significant data-export events. Security logs are monitored by the security operations function and retained for 365 days.
Critical vulnerabilities in internet-facing production systems are remediated within 20 calendar days. High vulnerabilities are remediated within 45 calendar days. Where remediation is not practical, a documented compensating control and risk acceptance are required.
NOTE: The remediation deadlines in this section intentionally differ from the policy baseline.

=== PAGE 6 ===
6. Security Incident Management

Northstar will investigate suspected events without unreasonable delay and take reasonable measures to contain and remediate confirmed Security Incidents.
Northstar will notify the Customer without undue delay and no later than ten calendar days after confirming that a Security Incident affected Customer Data. Initial notice may be supplemented as investigation continues.
- Notice will describe known impact and containment actions.
- Northstar will preserve relevant records and cooperate with reasonable Customer inquiries.

=== PAGE 7 ===
7. Audit and Independent Assurance

Northstar will provide its current independent security assurance report and a summary of material remediation activity upon written request once per year.
If the report identifies an unresolved material control concern relevant to the Customer, the Customer may conduct a focused audit with fifteen business days' notice. Audits must occur during business hours, protect other customers' information and avoid unreasonable disruption.
- The Customer bears its audit costs.
- Auditors must be independent and bound by confidentiality.

=== PAGE 8 ===
8. Retention, Return and Deletion

During the service term, online Customer Data is retained for the duration selected in the order form. If the order form does not state a duration, Northstar retains data for a reasonable operational period based on service needs.
After termination, Northstar will make Customer Data available for export for thirty days and will delete active copies within forty-five days after the export period. Encrypted backup copies expire through a documented ninety-day backup cycle.
NOTE: The active-copy deletion deadline and backup expiry are measurable; the in-term default retention phrase is intentionally vague.

=== PAGE 9 ===
9. Subprocessors

Northstar will bind each Material Subprocessor to written confidentiality, security and data-protection obligations no less protective than the relevant obligations in this Agreement.
Northstar will provide at least thirty days' advance notice before adding or replacing a Material Subprocessor. The Customer may object on reasonable documented security or privacy grounds during that period.
For an urgent service-restoration event, Northstar may appoint a temporary hosting provider before notice, provided notice is issued within five business days and the temporary provider is removed or regularized promptly.
NOTE: The urgent exception creates a controlled conflict for review.

=== PAGE 10 ===
10. Data Location and Transfers

Primary production processing occurs in Canada and Ireland. Support personnel in approved locations may access Customer Data when necessary to resolve incidents or customer requests.
Northstar will use contractual and technical safeguards for international transfers. The Customer may request the current list of processing locations but the Agreement does not require Customer approval before a support location changes.
- Transfer assessments are reviewed periodically.
- Remote support access is logged and subject to privileged-access controls.

=== PAGE 11 ===
11. Availability and Service Continuity

Northstar targets 99.9 percent monthly production availability, excluding agreed maintenance and Customer-caused outages.
Northstar maintains documented business continuity and disaster recovery plans and performs an exercise at least annually. The hosted service has an eight-hour recovery time objective and four-hour recovery point objective.
- The Customer receives an annual exercise summary on request.
- Material recovery-plan changes are reviewed by service leadership.

=== PAGE 12 ===
12. Term, Liability and Order of Precedence

The initial term is twelve months and renews under the applicable order form. Either party may terminate for uncured material breach after thirty days' written notice.
If documents conflict, the Data Protection Schedule controls for processing terms, the Security Schedule controls for technical safeguards, and the main Agreement controls for other matters.
