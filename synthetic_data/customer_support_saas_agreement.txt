=== PAGE 2 ===
2. Processing and Confidentiality

BrightDesk processes Customer Data only to deliver, secure and support the subscribed service or as documented by the Customer. Personnel with access are bound by confidentiality.
Aggregated service statistics may be used to improve reliability if the statistics do not identify the Customer or an individual.
- Customer configures user roles.
- BrightDesk documents material processing activities.

=== PAGE 3 ===
3. Encryption and Network Security

Customer Data is encrypted at rest using AES-256. Backup media and replicated production storage use the same or equivalent approved encryption strength.
External service endpoints require TLS 1.2 or later. Internal service traffic carrying Customer Data is encrypted using authenticated transport controls.
- Encryption keys are access-controlled.
- Deprecated ciphers are removed through the security baseline process.

=== PAGE 4 ===
4. Identity and Access Management

BrightDesk assigns unique workforce identities and uses role-based access with least privilege. Production access is reviewed every three months.
Privileged and remote access requires multi-factor authentication. Emergency access is logged, time-bound and reviewed by a separate approver.
- Shared human accounts are prohibited.
- Inactive access is disabled automatically.

=== PAGE 5 ===
5. Security Logging and Monitoring

BrightDesk logs authentication, privilege changes, administrative actions, security alerts and material exports. Logs are monitored through the security operations process.
Security logs are retained for 200 days in searchable storage and then archived or securely removed according to the logging standard.
- Clock synchronization is maintained.
- Alert rules are reviewed periodically.

=== PAGE 6 ===
6. Vulnerability and Security Testing

BrightDesk performs automated vulnerability scanning and an independent penetration test at least annually.
Critical vulnerabilities are targeted for remediation within fifteen calendar days. High vulnerabilities are ordinarily remediated within thirty calendar days, but the Security Officer may approve a documented extension when service stability requires additional testing.
NOTE: The extension mechanism makes high-severity remediation conditional.

=== PAGE 7 ===
7. Security Incidents

BrightDesk will notify the Customer without undue delay and within seventy-two hours after confirming a Security Incident affecting Customer Data.
BrightDesk will provide available details, containment actions and material updates. Final investigation reports are provided when reasonably complete.
- The Customer controls its own external notifications.
- Both parties preserve relevant evidence.

=== PAGE 8 ===
8. Audit and Assurance

BrightDesk will provide a current independent assurance report and penetration-test executive summary once per year on request.
Customer may submit reasonable follow-up questions. Direct audits are permitted only if required by a regulator or if the assurance report identifies a material unresolved control issue.
NOTE: Direct audit exists, but only in limited circumstances.

=== PAGE 9 ===
9. Data Retention

The Customer configures ticket retention between 30 and 730 days. Data reaching the configured limit is queued for deletion within seven days.
System-generated operational metadata is retained for 180 days unless a shorter legal or customer requirement applies.
- Legal holds are documented and limited to the required data.
- Retention settings are visible to authorized Customer administrators.

=== PAGE 10 ===
10. Return, Deletion and Backups

After termination, Customer may export Customer Data for thirty days. BrightDesk deletes active Customer Data within forty-five days after termination.
Encrypted backup copies are isolated from routine access and removed through a sixty-day rolling backup cycle. Restored backup data is re-applied to the deletion process.
- Deletion confirmation is available on request.

=== PAGE 11 ===
11. Subprocessors

BrightDesk maintains a published list of Material Subprocessors and imposes written confidentiality, security and data-protection obligations on each.
BrightDesk provides at least thirty days' advance email notice before a material new Subprocessor processes Customer Data. The Customer may object on documented data-protection grounds.
- BrightDesk remains responsible for contracted Subprocessor performance.

=== PAGE 12 ===
12. Processing Locations and Transfers

Primary hosting occurs in the United States and Germany. BrightDesk may provide support from any country in which BrightDesk or an affiliate maintains an approved support center.
Contractual safeguards apply to international transfers, but the Customer cannot restrict a support location or terminate solely because an additional support country is introduced.
NOTE: The broad location right intentionally creates a policy concern.

=== PAGE 13 ===
13. Availability and Continuity

BrightDesk targets 99.95 percent monthly service availability, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least forty-eight hours in advance.
BrightDesk maintains continuity and disaster recovery plans, exercises them annually, and sets a six-hour recovery time objective and two-hour recovery point objective for the hosted service.
- Exercise summaries are available on request.
- Material lessons are tracked to completion.

Security commitments in Sections 3 through 13 form the Security Appendix and may not be materially reduced during the subscription term.
